Beyond black holes

Black holes have cosmic cousins. Here is the first one you can play with.

Pulsars: cosmic lighthouses

When a big star explodes, its core can collapse into a neutron star: more mass than the Sun packed into a ball about the size of a city. Some spin hundreds of times a second and sweep beams across space. Those are pulsars.
