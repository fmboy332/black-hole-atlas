Orbital periods use the real values, so the relative speeds are true. Orbit distances are drawn on a compressed (square-root) scale so Neptune fits on screen. "Sizes: true scale" draws every body at its true size relative to the others (the inner planets become tiny next to the Sun); "exaggerated" boosts small bodies so you can see them. Colours are simple illustrations. Drag to orbit, scroll/pinch to zoom; buttons and Tab/Enter work from the keyboard. Moons without orbit numbers are listed with facts only. Space is silent; for sonifications see Sounds.

Illustrations and simulations are not photographs. Sounds are labelled as recordings or sonifications; space itself carries no sound.
