Numbers: NASA Exoplanet Archive composite values, checked by Oraly; they can be revised. "Not measured" means the archive lists no value (for example, no radius for planets never seen transiting). Confidence tags: established, interpretation, debated, candidate, hypothesis. Space is silent; for sonifications see Sounds, and for the weirdest worlds see Strange Worlds.

Illustrations and simulations are not photographs. Sounds are labelled as recordings or sonifications; space itself carries no sound.
