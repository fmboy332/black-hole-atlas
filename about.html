About

The UniVast is a free site for exploring the universe in 3D, from our Solar System out to the observable universe.

What is here

  • 3D viewers for the Solar System, exoplanets, black holes, pulsars and a black hole and neutron star merger.
  • Fact pages for galaxies and other objects, with a tag on each statement showing how settled it is.
  • Sounds, each labelled as a recording (spacecraft radio waves converted to audio) or a sonification (data turned into sound), with credit and licence.

How we keep it honest

  • Facts come from public sources listed on the Sources page.
  • Simulations and illustrations are not photographs, and speculation is labelled as such.
  • Distances are approximate and scientific values get revised.
  • Space itself carries no sound; see the Sounds page for what each clip really is.
