How we label things

  • Established: widely accepted, measured or well modelled.
  • Interpretation: the data are real, but the explanation is one reading of them.
  • Debated: scientists disagree.
  • Candidate / hypothesis: not confirmed; a proposed idea.
  • Illustration: a drawing or simulation, not a photograph.

About sound

Space itself carries no sound, because sound needs a medium. Every clip on this site is labelled a recording (for example radio signals played as audio) or a sonification (data turned into sound). The credit and licence for each clip are listed on the Sounds page.

Honest limits

  • Exoplanet values are the NASA Exoplanet Archive's composite best values and get revised. Masses are often minimum masses. Equilibrium temperatures are calculated, not measured.
  • Distances to many objects are approximate.
  • Sizes and colours in 3D scenes are illustrative.

Illustrations and simulations are not photographs. Sounds are labelled as recordings or sonifications; space itself carries no sound.
