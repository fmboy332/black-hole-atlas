Glossary

Plain-language definitions of space words.

Black hole
A region where gravity is so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape.
Event horizon
The boundary of no return around a black hole.
Schwarzschild radius
The horizon radius of a non-spinning black hole, 2GM/c², about 2.95 km per solar mass.
Singularity
The predicted point of infinite density at the center. Our physics likely breaks down there.
Accretion disk
A hot, glowing disk of gas spiraling around a black hole.
ISCO
Innermost stable circular orbit. Matter closer than this spirals in.
Photon sphere
The region where light can orbit a black hole unstably.
Spin (a)
How fast a black hole rotates, as a number from 0 to 1.
Frame dragging
A spinning mass drags spacetime around with it.
Tidal force
The difference in gravity across an object, which stretches it.
Spaghettification
Being stretched into a long thin shape by tidal forces.
Supernova
The explosion of a massive star at the end of its life.
Gravitational waves
Ripples in spacetime, for example from merging black holes.
Hawking radiation
Predicted faint radiation from black holes. Not yet observed.
Solar mass
The mass of our Sun, a unit for measuring stars and black holes.
Pulsar
A fast-spinning neutron star that sends beams of radiation. Covered later in the site.
