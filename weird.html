Weird Space Stuff

Short fun facts by Mr. Fart.

Pulsars: dead stars that spin like a lighthouse. The fastest known one spins about 716 times every second.
Neutron stars: a teaspoon of one would weigh around a billion tonnes or more. Do not put it in your coffee.
Black hole sound: NASA turned pressure waves around a black hole in the Perseus galaxy cluster into sound, about 57 octaves below middle C. You'd never hear it. Lucky you.
Spaghettification: yes, that's the real scientific word. Physicists looked at a catastrophe and said "pasta."
